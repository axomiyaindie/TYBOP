# AI Prompt Starter Pack

**Business name ideas**
> I run a [type of business] in [place]. My customers are [who]. Suggest 15 short, memorable names that are easy to spell and have a .com or .in domain likely available. Explain each in one line.

**Product descriptions**
> Write 3 short, friendly product descriptions for [product]. Audience: [who]. Tone: warm, honest, no hype. Include one benefit and one detail.

**Customer questions**
> List the 15 questions a first-time customer would ask before buying [product/service], with clear short answers I can reuse on WhatsApp and my website.

**Content calendar**
> Create a 30-day Instagram content plan for [business]. Mix education, behind-the-scenes, and offers. Give each post a hook and a caption idea.
