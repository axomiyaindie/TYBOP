## What is TYBOP?
**TYBOP** stands for **Take Your Business Online Playbook**. It's a free, open-source guide that shows you how to get a business online and grow it using today's tools, including AI.

You don't need to be technical. You don't need a big budget. You need a business (or an idea) and a few hours a week.

## How to use each chapter
Every chapter follows the same shape:

1. **What you'll get**: the outcome
2. **Why it matters**: the reasoning, in plain words
3. **Steps**: what to do, in order
4. ** AI shortcut**: a prompt or workflow that saves time
5. **Free / low-cost tools**: starting points, not endorsements
6. **Common mistakes**: so you can skip them
7. ** Do this today**: one small action, under 30 minutes

> **Tip:** Don't read the whole playbook first. Read one chapter, do its "Do this today", then move on. Progress beats planning.

## Golden rules
- **Start small.** One product, one channel, one city is enough.
- **Be reachable.** A customer who can't contact you will go elsewhere.
- **Trust is your product.** Real photos, clear prices, honest policies.
- **Use AI as a helper, not a replacement for your judgment.** You know your customers; the AI doesn't.
- **Keep records.** Orders, money in, money out. Future you will say thanks.
