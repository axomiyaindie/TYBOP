# 04 · Your Online Home

## What you'll get
A findable, trustworthy online presence: a domain name, a Google Business Profile, and a simple website, all set up in a way you control.

## Why it matters
When someone hears about your business, the first thing they do is search for it. If they find nothing, or something outdated, many will move on to a competitor.

Your online home does three jobs:

1. **Be found.** People can discover you on Google Search and Maps.
2. **Build trust.** Real photos, clear information and reviews show you are real.
3. **Be reachable.** One tap to call, message or buy.

It also gives you something social media can't: **ownership**. Followers on a platform belong to the platform. Your domain and website belong to you.

## The three levels

You don't need everything on day one. Start at Level 1 and move up when you're ready.

| Level | What you set up | Best for | Time |
|-------|-----------------|----------|------|
| **1. Basic presence** | Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business, one social profile | Local shops and services, testing an idea | 1 to 2 hours |
| **2. Simple website** | Domain plus a one-page site with contact buttons | Anyone who wants to look credible and be searchable | A weekend |
| **3. Full website or store** | Multi-page site or online shop (see chapter 05) | Businesses with many products or online orders | 1 to 2 weeks |

**Which should I start with?**

| If you... | Start with |
|-----------|-----------|
| Serve customers in one area (shop, salon, clinic, tutor) | Level 1, then Level 2 |
| Sell a few products through chat or Instagram | Level 1, then a simple catalog page |
| Offer a service anywhere (freelancer, consultant, agency) | Level 2 first |
| Plan to take orders and payments online | Level 2, then jump to chapter 05 |

## Steps

### Step 1: Fix your basic details
Decide these once and use them **everywhere**. Inconsistent details confuse customers and search engines.

- Business name (exactly as you'll write it)
- Phone or WhatsApp number
- Address or service area
- Opening hours
- One-line description of what you do
- Logo and 5 to 10 real photos

### Step 2: Set up your Google Business Profile (free)
This is the single most useful free step for any local business. It puts you on Google Search and Maps.

1. Search for "Google Business Profile" and sign in with a Google account that **you** own, not a friend's or freelancer's.
2. Add your business name, category, address or service area, phone and hours.
3. Add your website link once you have one.
4. Upload real photos: front of the shop, inside, products, you at work.
5. Write a clear description (use the AI shortcut below).
6. Complete verification. Google decides which options you get (for example phone, email, video or a postcard), and this varies by business and location.
7. Add your services or products with short descriptions.
8. Share your review link with happy customers and reply to every review.

> Check Google's own help pages for the current steps. The screens and verification options change from time to time.

### Step 3: Set up WhatsApp Business (free)
If your customers use WhatsApp, this is your front desk.

- Create a business profile with your logo, hours, address and description
- Add a **catalog** of your products or services
- Set a greeting message and an away message
- Save **quick replies** for common questions (price, delivery, location)
- Use **labels** to track new customers, pending orders and paid orders

### Step 4: Buy your domain name
A domain is your web address, such as `yourbusiness.in` or `yourbusiness.com`.

**Choosing a good one**
- Short and easy to say aloud
- Easy to spell, with no hyphens or confusing numbers
- Matches your business name and social handles
- Prefer `.com` for global reach or `.in` for an India-focused business
- Check that the name isn't already used by a similar business

**Protecting it**
- Register it in **your own name and email**, never only a freelancer's
- Turn on **auto-renew** so you don't lose it
- Turn on two-factor authentication on the registrar account
- Keep a note of where you bought it and which email owns it
- Use a reputable registrar and compare the yearly renewal price, not only the first-year offer

### Step 5: Build a simple website
Start with one page that answers a visitor's questions quickly.

**What every simple site needs**

| Section | What to include |
|---------|----------------|
| Headline | What you do and who it's for, in one sentence |
| Proof | Real photos, short testimonials, past work, number of customers |
| Offer | Your products or services and how they work |
| Price | Prices, or a clear "starting from" or how to get a quote |
| Contact | Phone, WhatsApp and email buttons that work on mobile |
| Location | Address, map link, hours or service area |
| Trust | About you, policies (refund, delivery) and your registration details if you have them |

**Tools to build it**

| Option | Good for | Trade-off |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| Google Sites | Quick, free, very simple pages | Limited design and features |
| Carrd | Clean one-page sites | Best for single pages only |
| Wix, Squarespace, WordPress.com | Drag-and-drop with more design options | Monthly fee for custom domain and features |
| Shopify or similar | Selling many products online | Costs more, built for stores (see chapter 05) |
| GitHub Pages | Free hosting if you're comfortable with code | Needs some technical skill |

Pick one and finish a first version. You can move later because you own the domain.

### Step 6: Make it easy to find
- Use your business name, what you do and your city in the page title
- Write a short, honest description of the page
- Compress photos so the page loads quickly on a phone
- Check it on a mobile phone first, since most visitors will use one
- Link your site from Google Business Profile, WhatsApp, Instagram and your email signature
- Use Google Search Console (free) to see how your site appears in search

### Step 7: Get a business email
`hello@yourbusiness.com` looks more trustworthy than a personal address and keeps your customer messages in one place. Most domain providers and suites like Google Workspace offer this for a small monthly fee.

## AI shortcut

**Google Business Profile description**
> Write a clear 700-character description for a [type of business] in [area]. We offer [services]. Our customers are [who]. Mention one thing that makes us different. Plain language, no exaggeration.

**Website outline**
> I run a [business] in [place]. Suggest a one-page website structure with section headings and a short draft for each. Include a headline, three benefits, a section for common questions and a clear call to action. Keep it simple and honest.

**Domain ideas**
> Suggest 20 short domain name ideas for [business] in [place]. They should be easy to spell and say aloud. Avoid hyphens and numbers.

**Customer FAQ**
> Write 8 questions and answers a first-time customer might have about [business]. Mark anything where I must add my real prices, dates or policies.

Always replace placeholders with your real details. Check every fact before publishing.

## Free and low-cost starting points

| Need | Free option | Paid or later |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Local listing | Google Business Profile | Not needed |
| Customer chat | WhatsApp Business | WhatsApp Business Platform (for larger teams) |
| Simple site | Google Sites, Carrd free plan | Paid plans for custom domain and extras |
| Domain | None, a small yearly cost | Needed for credibility |
| Business email | Forwarding from your domain | Google Workspace or similar |
| Photos | Your own phone camera | A photographer, once you're growing |
| Design | Canva free plan | Canva paid or a designer |

Prices change, so check current plans before you buy.

## Common mistakes
- **Using a personal account to own everything.** If a freelancer registers your domain or Google profile under their account, you may lose access later.
- **Different names and numbers in different places.** Keep them identical everywhere.
- **Stock photos only.** Real photos build trust. Use stock images sparingly.
- **Waiting for a perfect website.** A simple live page beats a perfect page that never launches.
- **Hiding contact details.** Make phone and WhatsApp buttons obvious.
- **Ignoring reviews.** Reply to every review, including the negative ones, politely.
- **Forgetting to renew.** A lapsed domain can send your customers to a stranger's page.

## Launch checklist

- [ ] Business details written down and consistent
- [ ] Google Business Profile created and verified
- [ ] WhatsApp Business set up with catalog and quick replies
- [ ] Domain bought in my own name, with auto-renew on
- [ ] Simple website live with contact buttons working on mobile
- [ ] Website link added to Google Business Profile and social profiles
- [ ] Business email created
- [ ] First 5 real photos uploaded
- [ ] First review requested from a happy customer

1. Write down your business name, phone number, address and hours in one note.
2. Create or claim your **Google Business Profile**.
3. Upload three real photos of your business.
