# 07 · The AI Toolkit

## What you'll get
A practical set of AI workflows you can use this week to save hours on writing, design, research and customer replies, without losing your personal touch.

## Why it matters
Small businesses are usually one to five people doing everything. AI can act like a fast, always-available assistant for the repetitive parts: first drafts, ideas, summaries, replies. That frees you for what only you can do: building relationships and making decisions.

But AI also makes mistakes confidently. The winning approach is **AI drafts, you decide**.

## What AI is good at (and not)

| Good for | Be careful with |
|------------|--------------------|
| First drafts of posts, descriptions, emails | Facts, prices, laws, tax rules: always verify |
| Brainstorming names, offers, ideas | Anything that must sound exactly like *you* without editing |
| Summarising long text or customer feedback | Sharing customer data or private documents |
| Translating and simplifying language | Medical, legal or financial claims |
| Organising messy notes into a plan | Replacing real photos of your real products |

## The five-job toolkit

### 1.  Writing assistant
Product descriptions, captions, emails, FAQs, WhatsApp replies.

> **Prompt:** *"I sell [product] to [audience] in [place]. Write 3 product descriptions, each under 60 words. Tone: warm and honest. Mention one benefit and one real detail. Don't use words like 'revolutionary' or 'best-in-class'."*

### 2.  Design helper
Logos, banners, posters, social graphics. Use AI to *explore* looks, then finish in a simple design tool.

> **Prompt:** *"Suggest 5 colour palettes and 2 font pairings for a [type] brand that feels [3 adjectives]. Give hex codes and say why each works."*

Always use **real photos** of your products for selling. Use AI images only for backgrounds, mood and decoration, and say so if it could mislead.

### 3.  Research buddy
Competitor scans, customer questions, pricing ideas, market gaps.

> **Prompt:** *"List 10 questions customers ask before buying [product]. For each, give a short answer I can verify. Mark anything that depends on my location or on current rules."*

Then check the answers yourself. Treat AI research as a starting point.

### 4.  Customer-reply assistant
Speed up repeated questions while staying human.

> **Prompt:** *"Here are 10 messages customers sent me [paste, with names removed]. Group them by topic and write a short, friendly reply template for each. Keep each under 40 words."*

Save the best replies as WhatsApp Business quick replies.

### 5.  Operations helper
Checklists, SOPs, meeting notes, weekly plans.

> **Prompt:** *"Turn these rough notes into a step-by-step checklist for packing and shipping an order: [paste notes]. Add a 'check before sending' list."*

## A 60-minute weekly AI routine

| Time | Task |
|------|------|
| 10 min | Brainstorm this week's 5 post ideas |
| 20 min | Draft captions and edit them into your own voice |
| 10 min | Update FAQs or reply templates from this week's questions |
| 10 min | Summarise customer feedback; pick one thing to improve |
| 10 min | Plan next week's offer or promotion |

## Write better prompts: the CRAFT formula

- **C**ontext: who you are and who it's for
- **R**ole: "Act as a friendly local shop assistant..."
- **A**sk: exactly what you want
- **F**ormat: length, list or paragraph, tone
- **T**est: "Ask me 3 questions first if anything is unclear."

## Safety and honesty checklist
- [ ] Don't paste customer phone numbers, addresses or payment details into AI tools
- [ ] Verify every price, date, claim and legal statement
- [ ] Edit AI text so it sounds like you
- [ ] Don't invent reviews, testimonials or fake customers
- [ ] Be clear when something is AI-generated if it could mislead people

## Free / low-cost tools
Tools change fast, so check current features and pricing yourself.
- **AI chat assistants** (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini): writing, research, planning
- **Canva**: simple design and templates
- **WhatsApp Business**: catalog, quick replies, labels
- **Google Business Profile**: free listing on Search and Maps
- **Google Sheets**: orders and expenses

## Common mistakes
- **Copy-pasting without editing.** Customers can tell, and it sounds generic.
- **Over-automating too early.** Learn what works manually first.
- **Trusting facts blindly.** Check anything that affects money or law.
- **Using too many tools.** Master one or two before adding more.
- **Forgetting the human.** People buy from people they trust.

## Do this today
1. Pick **one** of the five jobs above.
2. Run the matching prompt with your real business details.
3. Edit the result until it sounds like you, then use it.
4. Save your best prompt in a note. It's the start of your own prompt library.
